Wayloom

Wayloom is an iPhone app that turns your booking emails, PDFs and screenshots into one calm trip plan: flights, stays, parking, tours and ideas, in order, with reminders.

Common questions

How do I add a booking? On Today tap Import and choose an email, PDF or screenshot, or tap Type it in. You check everything before it is added.

How do I share a trip? Tap the gear, then Invite people. Everyone you invite needs iCloud.

Where is my data? On your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The developer cannot see it.

How do I delete a trip? Tap the briefcase, then the "…" next to the trip. Or tap the gear and use "Delete this trip" at the bottom.
