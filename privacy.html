Wayloom privacy policy

Who we are. Wayloom is made by Ciaran Burkett (ciaranburkett@me.com).

What Wayloom stores. The trips, bookings and notes you add. They are saved on your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud account, which only you (and people you choose to share with) can access. We cannot see them.

What Wayloom reads. Screenshots, PDFs, emails and files you choose to add. They are read on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to any third party.

Apple services. Wayloom asks Apple Maps to check a place name and address, and to suggest places as you type in a place box (only the words you type are sent), and Apple Weather for a forecast for your trip places. These requests go from your phone to Apple and are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases. Wayloom is free to try for 14 days, then a one-time purchase of 4 CHF unlocks adding and editing. The payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The start of your trial is kept in your iPhone Keychain, on the device only.

Calendar and notifications. Only if you turn them on. Calendar events are written to a calendar called Wayloom. Reminders are set on your phone.

What we do not do. No account, no advertising, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data, no analytics.

Your choices. Delete a trip or all content in the app at any time. Removing the app deletes the phone copy; delete the app's iCloud data in Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Children. Wayloom is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes. If this changes we will update this page and the app.
